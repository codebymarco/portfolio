How it works

From measurements to installed cladding — usually 3 to 5 weeks.
01

Measure

We measure your wall on-site, including outlets, switches, and any obstructions.

02

Design

Choose a layout and timber finish — we'll plan the panel sizing for a seamless fit.

03

Cut & finish

Slats and panels are cut, sanded, and finished in our Durban workshop.

04

Install

Fitted on-site by our team, with wiring and outlets worked around cleanly.
