Designing a feature wall in Durban

A feature wall works when it's designed as part of the room, not added on top of it. The best feature walls consider the wall's size, the light it gets, what's opposite it, and what the room is used for — then choose a material and layout that suits all of that, rather than just picking something that looks good on Pinterest.

Wallstudio designs and builds feature walls for homes across Durban — from bedrooms in Westville to lounges in Umhlanga and entranceways in Kloof. We work across wallpaper, timber cladding, wall art, and headboards, and can combine any of these into a single designed wall.

Popular feature wall combinations

  • Wallpaper mural with a matching upholstered headboard
  • Timber slat cladding with integrated floating shelves
  • Framed wall art arranged with a textured wallpaper backdrop
  • Herringbone timber panel as a standalone entrance statement
  • Cladding plus carved-frame mirror for hallways and landings

How we approach a feature wall brief

We start with the wall itself — its dimensions, light, and role in the room — before talking materials. From there we'll suggest one or two directions rather than overwhelming you with options, then refine based on your feedback before anything goes into production.
