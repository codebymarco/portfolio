Choosing a headboard designer in Durban

A headboard is one of the few pieces of furniture that's both functional and a design statement — it anchors the whole bedroom. The difference between an off-the-shelf headboard and a designed one comes down to fit, fabric, and proportion: a headboard built for your exact bed and wall will always look more intentional than a standard size squeezed into the gap.

Wallstudio designs and builds custom headboards from our Durban workshop, working with homeowners across the city and surrounding areas — from apartments in Umhlanga to family homes in Westville and Kloof. Every headboard is made to the measurements of your bed and wall, in a fabric and style you choose.

What "made to measure" actually means

  • Built to your exact bed width — single, double, queen, king, or super king
  • Height and proportions designed around your wall and ceiling
  • Choice of channel tufted, button tufted, arched, or flat-panel styles
  • Fabric from our range, or your own supplied material
  • Wall-mounted or freestanding, with fixings suited to your wall type

Why design matters as much as upholstery

A well-upholstered headboard in the wrong proportions still looks off. Before any fabric is cut, we think about how the headboard relates to your bed height, your wall width, and the rest of the room — so the finished piece looks like it was always meant to be there, not added afterwards.
